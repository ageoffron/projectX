About

I’m a principal systems engineer based in the Los Angeles area, with a career in aviation and aerospace infrastructure. The work sits where storage, data-center operations, Linux, and information security meet — the layer nobody notices until it fails.

I studied computer science at Telecom Lille 1 in France. Outside production systems I still tinker: amateur radio, bare-metal tooling, and the occasional hardware experiment.
