Santa Cruz Road Trip

Four days up the California coast from South Pasadena to Santa Cruz and back, November twenty-seventh through thirtieth, with a dog along for the ride.

  • 🚐 Camping van
  • 🐕 Dog-friendly
  • Nov 27–30, 2026
01

Overview

  • DepartFri Nov 27 · ~12:15 PM
  • Van pickupBy 1:00 PM
  • RouteUS-101 north

Depart South Pasadena around twelve fifteen, pick up the Indie Campers van at the Towne Avenue depot in South LA by one, then head north on US-one-oh-one. Door to door with the van pickup, plan on about four and a quarter hours to Morro Bay.

02

Day by day

South Pasadena → Morro Bay

Pick up the van, then roughly three hours north on US-one-oh-one to Morro Bay. Set up camp near the bay. Nothing booked yet — first reservation target is Morro Bay State Park for Friday night at thirty-five dollars.

Morro Bay → Santa Cruz

Morning along the coast, then up to Santa Cruz. Explore the beach, the wharf, and West Cliff Drive. Overnight at Sunset State Beach, thirty-five dollars.

Santa Cruz → Morro Bay

Head back south and spend Sunday night in Morro Bay again — thirty-five dollars. Shorter Sunday options if you want less driving: San Simeon and Cambria, or Pismo Beach with sites from twenty-five.

Morro Bay → South Pasadena

Return the van Monday. West Cliff overnight parking is not legal, New Brighton and Seacliff are full, and Harbor RV Park won't take a small van without a built-in toilet.

03

Shorter Sunday options

San Simeon · Cambria

Stop at San Simeon instead of Morro Bay to shorten Sunday's drive.

Pismo Beach

Sites from twenty-five dollars.
