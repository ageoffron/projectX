Dog-Friendly · Coastal Trails

Dog Hikes

Trail options for the sixty-pound pup along the Santa Cruz coast, checked against leash rules and seasonal access.

01

Trails

West Cliff Drive

Paved coastal path from Natural Bridges to the wharf. Leash required. No overnight van parking along it.

Wilder Ranch

Coastal bluff trails south of town. Day-use fee. Leash on.

Henry Cowell Redwoods

Redwood groves inland. Popular with dogs on leash; arrive early for parking.

02

Notes

  • Leash on every trail listed.
  • Check Santa Cruz Trails before you go — seasonal closures change fast.
  • Bring water; coastal fog rolls in quick even in late November.
