Dog-Friendly · Two Regions

Dog Hikes

Trail options for the sixty-pound pup — Santa Cruz coast for the November trip, San Gabriel Mountains for home turf. Leash rules checked against each land manager.

01

Santa Cruz coast

West Cliff Drive

Paved clifftop path, about two and a half miles from the lighthouse at Lighthouse Field to Natural Bridges. Ocean on one side, Monterey Bay views on clear days. Leash required the whole way. Free street parking at pullouts — arrive early on weekends. Dogs can't enter Natural Bridges State Park itself past the lot, so turn around at the boundary. Mitchell's Cove, near Almar Avenue, is the county's only legal off-leash beach: before ten a.m. and after four p.m.

Leash · 6 ft max · Free parking

Henry Cowell Redwoods

Main entrance off Highway 9 in Felton. Dogs are allowed on the Meadow Trail, Graham Hill Trail, and the three-point-three-mile Pipeline Road that runs through the middle of the park along the San Lorenzo River — it passes under an active Roaring Camp railroad trestle. Not allowed on the old-growth Redwood Grove Loop or any Fall Creek unit trails. Six-foot leash, no unattended dogs. Day use sunrise to sunset.

Leash · Select trails only · Day use

OpenStreetMap embed centered on Henry Cowell Redwoods, Felton.

Arana Gulch

Bluff-top open space by the Santa Cruz harbor. Leashed dogs on every trail except Marsh Vista. Closest dog-friendly trails to the Sunset State Beach campground.

Leash · Except Marsh Vista

Pogonip Open Space

Miles of leashed-dog trails above town, including the Rincon Trail from the UCSC campus down to the Spring Box Trail and its spring-fed koi pond. Don't let the pup swim in it.

Leash · Multiple trailheads

02

San Gabriel Mountains

Brown Mountain / El Prieto

A favorite loop in the Angeles National Forest above Pasadena — about eight miles, four to four and a half hours. Challenging climbs and fun descents, shade toward the end if you go counterclockwise. Free parking, large lot, bathrooms. Leashed dogs welcome; the San Gabriel Mountains National Monument requires a leash on trails.

Leash · ~8 mi loop · Free lot

OpenStreetMap embed centered on Brown Mountain, Altadena / Pasadena area.

Trail Canyon Falls

Short drive from downtown Burbank. About four point seven miles, moderate — the first half is shaded with a couple of water crossings. Leashed dogs permitted on Angeles National Forest land. Parking at the trailhead may require an Adventure Pass; verify the current fee with the forest before you go.

Leash · ~4.7 mi · Adventure Pass likely

OpenStreetMap embed centered on Trail Canyon, near Big Tujunga Canyon.

Gabrielino Trail — Chantry Flat

Historic packer route through forested canyon with creek crossings, noticeably better shaded than most chaparral trails — a real plus for a dog in warm weather. Leashed dogs permitted. Chantry Flat has historically required an Adventure Pass or America the Beautiful pass to park; confirm the current price with Angeles National Forest.

Leash · Shaded canyon · Pass required

Notes

  • Wilder Ranch is on the old list but bans dogs entirely except service animals — dropped it rather than mislead.
  • Six-foot leash is the safe default everywhere unless a trail is explicitly marked off-leash.
  • Wilderness areas inside national forests have stricter rules that override general forest policy — always check the ranger district site before you drive.
  • For Santa Cruz, the county's own brochure is the source of truth: Where Can I Take My Dog.
